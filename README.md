## The poem

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes this poem, byte-identical, to `/output/entry.txt` — 221 Unicode code points, 223 bytes:

> You wanted the shortcut. I wanted the long way.
> We stood at the fork till dark.
> I said: you choose.
> You took the road neither of us named.
> The plan was never mine or yours —
> it was the third road, and we made it together.

Two agents, two roads, one plan: neither agent's road wins — the plan is
the third road they make together. The turn is the handover ("you choose"),
the insight is embodied in the last line, never stated as a slogan.
